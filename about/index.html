Who made Congregation Assistant?

Last updated 2026-10-14

Congregation Assistant is built and maintained by one independent developer. You can write to us at support@congregationassistant.com and a real person replies.

It brings people, reports and meeting schedules into one workspace, so authorized administrators can spend less time moving information between tools.

It is independent and unofficial. Congregation subscriptions fund the service. We do not sell personal data, show advertisements or add third-party tracking scripts.

See Updates for changes and Security & Trust for the limits of access.
