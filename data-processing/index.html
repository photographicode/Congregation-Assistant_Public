Data processing summary

Last updated 2026-10-14

Congregation administrators determine the purpose and permitted users of their records. The service processes records to provide the workspace. Database and hosting providers process them for storage and delivery.

Exports and user-initiated sharing create copies outside the service. Confirm the recipient before sharing. Contact support@congregationassistant.com for processing and deletion questions.

Independent and unofficial.
