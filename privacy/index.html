Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-14

This workspace stores congregation membership, contact details, reporting and attendance records, assignments and account permissions for authorized administration. Administrators decide what to enter and who receives access.

Google account identity is used for sign-in. Supabase processes stored records. Hosting providers process requests needed to deliver the application. We do not add advertising or third-party tracking scripts, sell congregation records or use them to train AI.

We set no tracking cookies. Account session storage and local preferences are necessary for the app. Encrypted backup downloads stay under your control.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, export, withdrawal of consent or deletion through your congregation administrator and support@congregationassistant.com. If consent is withdrawn, the administrator must consider which records can lawfully be retained. Contact the same address for grievances or unresolved requests.

Monthly service reports retain the current and previous complete service year. Other retention and deletion require confirmation across the active database and provider backups. No automatic backup-deletion deadline is promised before those controls are verified.

India’s DPDP framework and applicable GDPR rights inform these choices. Your administrator must have an appropriate lawful purpose and authorization for every record.
