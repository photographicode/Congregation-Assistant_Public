Start with one task

Last updated 2026-10-17

  1. Ask your administrator to approve your Google account.
  2. Open People to add a publisher and verify the group and email.
  3. Open Midweek or Weekend to assign a meeting, then review before publishing.
  4. Open Reports to enter the month’s records.
  5. Open Print & PDF, select a document and generate its preview.
  6. Download or share only after checking the document.

Try these steps with fictional demo records. Contact support@congregationassistant.com if a task is unclear.
